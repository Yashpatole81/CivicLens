13Dashboard, queue, and complaint management

Counts come from real stored data, with a "Demo data" badge for seeded rows. Overdue complaints are sorted to the top using the SLA (Critical 24 h · High 3 d · Medium 7 d · Low 14 d). "Mark resolved" needs a resolution note. The AI suggestion and the final values are shown side by side.

Changed from the referenceNo "Continue as Guest". Neutral skyline instead of Stockholm. Phone is optional. Country, State/UT, and City pickers added. 6 issue groups replace the 4 quick categories. An Overdue KPI and a destination-city box are new.
Design languageWarm paper with a survey-map grid, deep civic green, and status chips that always carry a text label, not just color. Fraunces for headings, Public Sans for the UI, IBM Plex Mono for references and timestamps.
ScopeStatic mockup only: no app code and no real data. Names, emails, and photos are placeholders. Pune is just an example city. Accessibility still needs testing on a device with TalkBack.
